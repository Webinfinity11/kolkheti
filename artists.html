Artists

Six authors whose work belongs to the MAISON KOLKHETI collection. Each of them reads the myth of Colchis in their own language.

06Artists 2018—2025Period TbilisiOn view
01
I

Nino Beridze

Oil on canvas · 2019—2024

Large-format landscapes in which the Colchian marshland turns to golden haze. Colour goes on in layers — a single canvas can take her the better part of a year.

02
II

Levan Kavtaradze

Mixed media · 2021—2025

Metal leaf and pigment worked together on canvas. His series "Argonauts" turns the reflection on the sea into a physical surface.

03
III

Ana Mikeladze

Watercolour on paper · 2022—2025

Small formats, closer to diary entries than to pictures. She returns to one motif again and again until the form comes apart.

04
IV

Giorgi Lomidze

Acrylic on wood · 2020—2024

He works on salvaged doors and boards — the material is part of the piece. The splits in the wood stay on as lines in the composition.

05
V

Tamar Chkheidze

Tempera on paper · 2018—2023

She carries the technique of icon painting into contemporary portraiture. For her the gold ground is a historical quotation, not decoration.

06
VI

Davit Nadiradze

Charcoal on canvas · 2023—2025

The monochrome series "Birth of the Universe". He takes light out of darkness — the canvas is blacked out first, then the form is cleared back into it.
